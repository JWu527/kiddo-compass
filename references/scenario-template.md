# Scenario Template Library

Use this file to keep scenario advice consistent. Every scenario card should have four layers:

1. Quick help: what to say/do now.
2. Why it may be happening: short, non-shaming explanation.
3. Prevention: what to set up before next time.
4. If it does not work: next step or escalation threshold.

## Card template

```text
Scene:
Age band:
Caregiver:
Risk check:
Quick help:
Script:
Prevention:
If it fails:
Load:
```

## Public-beta scenario cards

These cards are short operating cards, not full content packs. Always run `safety-triage.md` first, then adjust the card by age band, caregiver role, and missing context.

### 1. Bedtime delay

```text
Scene: Asks for more stories, water, toys, or parent presence after bedtime.
Age band: 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent or grandparent.
Risk check: Check fear, illness, sleep debt, breathing concerns, or months-long severe disruption.
Quick help: Make the last step visible before it happens.
Script: "今晚还有最后一本。你选小熊这本，还是小车这本？讲完就关灯。"
Prevention: Add special time before bedtime and a visual bedtime routine.
If it fails: Use one calm repeat sentence; do not add new rewards. If sleep disruption is severe or persistent, move to yellow triage.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, routing-guide.md, scenario-guide.md.
```

### 2. Night waking or fear

```text
Scene: Wakes at night crying, asks for repeated interaction, or says afraid.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent or overnight caregiver.
Risk check: Check fever, pain, breathing, injury risk, night terrors, trauma, or caregiver exhaustion affecting safety.
Quick help: Keep lights and words low; offer calm presence without turning the wake-up into playtime.
Script: "我在。现在是睡觉时间。我拍拍你，等你安静下来。"
Prevention: Add daytime connection, predictable bedtime, and a short comfort object ritual.
If it fails: Track timing and triggers for one week; persistent severe sleep disruption needs professional input.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, safety-triage.md, scenario-guide.md.
```

### 3. Food throwing

```text
Scene: Throws food from table or high chair.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months.
Caregiver: Any mealtime caregiver.
Risk check: Check choking fear, vomiting, poor growth, extreme restriction, or feeding refusal.
Quick help: Stay calm, say the rule once, remove food if throwing continues.
Script: "食物是吃的，不是扔的。你还想吃就放桌上；继续扔，我就知道你吃饱了。"
Prevention: Smaller portions, end meal when full, avoid big adult reactions, and let the child help clean only when calm.
If it fails: Check hunger/fullness, texture, seat comfort, and caregiver reaction pattern; growth or feeding concerns move to yellow triage.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, scenario-guide.md.
```

### 4. Picky eating or refusing meals

```text
Scene: Refuses food, demands only one food, or turns meals into a power struggle.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent, nanny.
Risk check: Check growth, swallowing, vomiting, constipation, choking fear, medical restriction, or extreme food range.
Quick help: Separate adult job and child job.
Script: "我负责准备这些食物，你负责决定吃多少。你可以先闻一闻，也可以先不吃。"
Prevention: Offer one safe familiar food with new foods, keep portions tiny, and avoid pressure or bargaining.
If it fails: Track accepted foods and stress level; poor growth, pain, or extreme restriction needs pediatric guidance.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 5. Hitting or biting

```text
Scene: Hits, bites, kicks, or throws objects at people.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, teacher, grandparent.
Risk check: Block immediate harm; yellow/red triage for injury, escalating frequency, self-harm, cruelty, or adult loss of control.
Quick help: Stop the body first, teach one replacement after calm returns.
Script: "我不会让你打人。你很生气，可以跺脚，不能打。"
Prevention: Practice replacement actions when calm, reduce known triggers, and plan repair language.
If it fails: Track frequency, injury, trigger, and repair; months-long or injurious aggression needs professional evaluation.
Load: safety-triage.md, evidence-matrix.md.
```

### 6. Grabbing toys or not sharing

```text
Scene: Grabs toys, refuses to share, or melts down when another child touches belongings.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, teacher, grandparent.
Risk check: If aggression or injury appears, route to hitting/biting first.
Quick help: Protect ownership and turn-taking without moral labeling.
Script: "你还没准备好给。我们等你玩完，再轮到妹妹。妹妹现在可以选这辆车。"
Prevention: Put special toys away before playdates; use turn signals like timer or "when you are done."
If it fails: Reduce shared-toy pressure and practice trading when calm; repeated injury moves to safety triage.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 7. Tantrum or emotional meltdown

```text
Scene: Screaming, crying, lying on the floor, or unable to hear reasoning.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Any caregiver.
Risk check: Check injury risk, unsafe location, hunger, exhaustion, sensory overload, illness, or adult loss of control.
Quick help: Fewer words, lower body, calm presence, and one boundary.
Script: "你真的很想要。现在不能买。我在这里陪你哭一会儿。"
Prevention: Preview transitions, check snack/rest, and offer limited choices before the hard moment.
If it fails: Track duration and triggers; extreme, frequent, or escalating meltdowns may need professional input.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 8. Public-place meltdown

```text
Scene: Meltdown in supermarket, restaurant, park, transit, or family gathering.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent, multiple caregivers.
Risk check: First check traffic, crowd, choking, elopement, or adult anger.
Quick help: Move to a quieter/safe edge before teaching.
Script: "这里太吵了。我们先到旁边安全的地方。你可以哭，我会守着你。"
Prevention: Short trips, one clear mission, snack/rest check, and an exit plan.
If it fails: Shorten the outing next time and rehearse the first step at home; unsafe running or repeated injury needs triage.
Load: safety-triage.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 9. Daycare or school separation

```text
Scene: Cries, clings, screams, or refuses at drop-off.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent, teacher.
Risk check: Check trauma, bullying, harsh caregiver, regression, extreme panic, or inability to function.
Quick help: Keep goodbye short and reliable.
Script: "我抱你一下，然后我会走。午睡后我来接你。"
Prevention: Same goodbye ritual, transition object, teacher handoff, and predictable reunion.
If it fails: Ask duration and recovery time after parent leaves; extreme or persistent impairment moves to yellow triage.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 10. Toileting resistance

```text
Scene: Refuses potty, cries when invited, or fights toilet training.
Age band: 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent, daycare teacher.
Risk check: Check pain, constipation, blood, fear, urinary symptoms, or pressure/shame history.
Quick help: Lower pressure and check readiness.
Script: "身体准备好了会告诉我们。今天先坐一分钟，不出来也没关系。"
Prevention: Routine sit times, easy clothing, no shame, and small control choices.
If it fails: Pause pressure and track readiness; pain, constipation, blood, or frequent accidents after age 5 need professional input.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 11. Toileting accidents or regression

```text
Scene: New accidents after progress, hiding accidents, or shame around toileting.
Age band: 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, teacher.
Risk check: Check constipation, pain, fever, blood, stress, major life change, or accidents after age 5.
Quick help: Remove shame and make cleanup matter-of-fact.
Script: "裤子湿了。我们去换干净的。下次身体提醒你时，可以叫我。"
Prevention: Scheduled bathroom breaks, easy access, and neutral cleanup routine.
If it fails: Look for medical or stress triggers; frequent persistent accidents after age 5 need pediatric input.
Load: evidence-matrix.md, safety-triage.md.
```

### 12. Screen transition

```text
Scene: Screams, bargains, or grabs device when screen time ends.
Age band: 24-36 months, 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent.
Risk check: Check aggression, sleep disruption, and caregiver inconsistency.
Quick help: Preview ending and give a concrete after-screen action.
Script: "还有两分钟。结束后你选搭积木还是洗澡玩水。"
Prevention: Visual timer, same ending routine, charging place outside reach, and adult alignment.
If it fails: Change access rules before the next session instead of debating at every ending.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 13. Dressing or leaving the house refusal

```text
Scene: Refuses clothes, shoes, coat, car seat, or leaving home.
Age band: 12-24 months, 24-36 months, 3-5 years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent.
Risk check: Check sensory discomfort, pain, weather safety, or unsafe transport resistance.
Quick help: Offer two acceptable choices and keep the destination boundary clear.
Script: "我们要出门了。你选蓝鞋还是白鞋？你选，或者我帮你选。"
Prevention: Prepare clothes the night before, build a picture routine, and add buffer time.
If it fails: Reduce choices and check sensory triggers; car-seat safety remains a non-negotiable safety boundary.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 14. Morning rush

```text
Scene: Dawdling, resisting steps, or repeated conflict before school/work.
Age band: 24-36 months, 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, multiple caregivers.
Risk check: Check sleep debt, unrealistic timing, and adult yelling risk.
Quick help: Move from verbal reminders to a visible routine.
Script: "惯例表上下一步是什么？你指给我看。"
Prevention: Fewer morning decisions, prep the night before, and one connection moment before instructions.
If it fails: Remove one morning task from the schedule and review bedtime; recurring chaos is often a system problem, not only child behavior.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 15. Whining or repeated demands

```text
Scene: Repeats "I want", whines, or escalates after a no.
Age band: 24-36 months, 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent.
Risk check: Check hunger, tiredness, illness, and whether the demand masks connection needs.
Quick help: Acknowledge once, hold the limit, and show the acceptable way to ask.
Script: "你很想要。现在不能买。你可以用普通声音说：我很失望。"
Prevention: Predictable yes/no rules, special time, and practice asking when calm.
If it fails: Track when whining works; if adults sometimes give in after escalation, align the adult response first.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 16. Sibling conflict

```text
Scene: Fighting over attention, toys, fairness, or adult intervention.
Age band: 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, multiple caregivers.
Risk check: If injury, intimidation, or cruelty appears, route to safety triage.
Quick help: Stop harm, avoid instant judge mode, and name both needs.
Script: "我不会让你们伤害对方。你想要车，妹妹也想要车。我们先分开一下，再想办法。"
Prevention: Separate special time, fewer comparison words, and clear repair routines.
If it fails: Track patterns by time/trigger; persistent injury or fear moves to yellow triage.
Load: safety-triage.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 17. Lying or hiding mistakes

```text
Scene: Denies an obvious mistake, hides broken items, or blames others.
Age band: 3-5 years, 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, teacher.
Risk check: Check whether fear of punishment, shame, or unsafe adult reaction is driving hiding.
Quick help: Make truth safer than cover-up.
Script: "我看到杯子碎了。我们先处理玻璃。你告诉我发生了什么，我会帮你一起想办法。"
Prevention: Repair-focused consequences, adult apology modeling, and less interrogation.
If it fails: Look at the adult response after mistakes; if lying protects the child from harsh reactions, change the repair routine first.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 18. Homework or practice resistance

```text
Scene: Refuses homework, reading, piano, sports practice, or repeated learning tasks.
Age band: 6+ years.
Caregiver: Parent, teacher.
Risk check: Check learning difficulty, anxiety, sleep, unrealistic load, or humiliation around performance.
Quick help: Shrink the first step and give agency inside the boundary.
Script: "今天先做 10 分钟。你选先写语文还是先读英语。10 分钟后我们看下一步。"
Prevention: Predictable routine, visible progress, and problem-solving meeting when calm.
If it fails: Check skill mismatch and workload; ongoing distress may need teacher or professional assessment.
Load: routing-guide.md.
```

### 19. Grandparent disagreement

```text
Scene: Grandparents give candy/screens, rescue after limits, shame, threaten, or disagree with parents.
Age band: All.
Caregiver: Grandparent, parent, multiple caregivers.
Risk check: Separate safety boundaries from style preferences; abuse, humiliation, or unsafe care needs stronger action.
Quick help: Choose one shared bottom line, not a full philosophy debate.
Script: "我们想先统一一件事：睡前不再加糖和新动画。其他做法我们慢慢磨合。"
Prevention: Weekly one-card summary, appreciation first, and exact shared sentences.
If it fails: Protect safety boundaries first; keep flexible areas flexible to reduce power struggle among adults.
Load: grandparent-strategies.md, routing-guide.md.
```

### 20. Caregiver yelling and repair

```text
Scene: Adult yelled, threatened, grabbed, or feels close to losing control.
Age band: All.
Caregiver: Parent, grandparent, teacher.
Risk check: Red triage if there is immediate risk of hitting, shaking, locking up, starving, or serious harm.
Quick help: Create physical safety first, then repair with a short apology.
Script: "刚才我声音太大了，吓到你了。那是我的责任。我现在先冷静一下，等会儿我们再说规则。"
Prevention: Adult pause plan, tag-team handoff, fewer triggers during high-stress routines, and support for caregiver mental health.
If it fails: If the adult repeatedly feels out of control, recommend counselor/therapist or trusted support and prioritize safety.
Load: safety-triage.md, routing-guide.md.
```
