# Evidence Matrix

This matrix keeps Kiddo Compass from treating every issue as only a "discipline" problem. Use it as a calibration layer before scripts and tools.

## Source priority

1. Local safety and legal requirements for children's information and emergency risk.
2. Official health and child-development guidance.
3. positive parenting / Adlerian method sources.
4. Experience-based scripts and practice notes.

## Evidence labels

Use these labels internally when selecting advice:

- `official-consensus`: aligned with official public-health or child-development guidance.
- `research-review`: peer-reviewed systematic review or research synthesis; supports a category of practice, not every branded tool.
- `evidence-clearinghouse`: independent program-evidence rating (for example CEBC); treat as promising-or-stronger calibration, never as proof.
- `method-source`: positive parenting / Adlerian method guidance.
- `practice-pattern`: practical script from this skill's scenario library.
- `needs-evaluation`: professional evaluation may be needed.
- `experience-only`: useful as a parent script, but not a universal claim.

## Wording guardrails

Avoid result promises:

- Do not say: "do this for three days and the behavior will stop."
- Say: "try this consistently for a few days and watch whether the pattern changes."

Avoid reducing health/development issues to behavior:

- Do not say: "this is just seeking attention."
- Say: "this may be a connection need, but let's also check age, sleep, hunger, pain, and whether the pattern is escalating."
